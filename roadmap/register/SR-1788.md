A store to one element of a slice, `bytes[i] = v`, makes the element's address into an address slot and stores through it: x86-64 emits `leaq (%r8,%rsi), %r10` and `movb %r11b, (%r10)` where `movb %r11b, (%r8,%rsi)` would do. SHA-256's program spends 8 instructions a generated byte after R16.35 (D2367-D2369), one of them this `leaq`, 64 M of its 5.63 G at speed; every hosted loop that writes a slice's bytes does the same.

Completion: an address slot read only by the one indirect access after it folded into that access's addressing on x86-64, measured.
