The arm64 cross lane fails on epycmicro, at the item's base as well, with `runtime/events-network-limits` timed out at every profile: `./scripts/dev-test.sh --target=linux-arm64 --runner=qemu-aarch64 --suite='fixture execution'` reports "qemu-aarch64 ran longer than the limit of 120 seconds" six times, and `compiler/tests/cross-lanes.json`, which `docs/process.md` names as where the emulator's own failures are listed, has no row for it. Under `qemu-aarch64` the program sets `SO_RCVTIMEO` to 50 ms on its quiet sockets, takes `EAGAIN` from `recvfrom`, and then waits in `epoll_pwait` with no timeout until it is stopped; natively on x86-64 it passes. Met running the arm64 cross lane for R16.45 on 2026-11-28; the image the base compiler emits waits the same way.

Completion: the fixture passes under `qemu-aarch64`, or `cross-lanes.json` lists it with the emulator's reason, so the cross lane passes at a clean base.
