`python3 check.py` run while a merge of `origin/main` is being resolved refuses rows nobody touched: merging main into the nix-packages stage-2 candidate, with the conflicts resolved and staged but not yet committed, it named 14 rows (SR-03, SR-878, SR-901, R551-07 and others) as having "stood open through 11 completed items, more than 10" and asked for each to be scheduled or kept, while `origin/main` itself passed. Committing the merge, with nothing else changed, made the same check pass. `register_ages` treats a pending `MERGE_HEAD` differently from the committed merge, so the refusal says what to do about rows when the remedy is to commit first.

Completion: `check.py` gives the same register ages during a resolved but uncommitted merge as after committing it, or says the ages wait for the merge's commit.
