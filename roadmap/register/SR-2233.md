No lane says how long a program took, so a fixture getting close to a lane's time limit is not visible until it times out. `runtime/unicode-caseless-scalars` failed two gates that way and passed on each rerun: FreeBSD arm64's guest runs each program under `timeout 60` and keeps only its status, and the Ada harness keeps only "the program exceeded its execution time limit" against its 120 seconds. Splitting that fixture into four parts meant finding its times by hand: a temporary edit to `check.py`'s guest script printing `date +%s` around each program, and timing each binary under `qemu-riscv64` and `qemu-aarch64` with a script of its own, none of which a document leads to.

Completion: Each lane records every program's wall time beside its status in its retained evidence, and names the programs that took more than half of their limit, or the reason recorded.
