A hexadecimal float without a point, `0x1p-20` as C writes it, is refused as L0011 "this is not an integer any base spells", whose note speaks of digits and underscores and never says that Landin's hexadecimal float needs a point, `0x1.0p-20` [0220]. R22.10's trigonometric stream met it porting msun's constants.

Completion: a literal that reads as a hexadecimal float without its point is refused with a note that shows the spelling with the point, or `refine fix` rewrites it.
