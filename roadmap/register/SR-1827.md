R16.36 timed boards on the rig with `environments/boards/timing.py remote` to compare with the gate, whose figure is the lower median of three runs of each image. `remote` and `run` default to `--repeat 1`, so a run by hand gives one sample a gate run would not, and nothing says the two differ; a figure that moved by a sample's noise looked like a change until the runs were repeated with `--repeat 3`.

Completion: `run` and `remote` take the gate's three runs unless told otherwise, or say that one run is not the gate's figure.
