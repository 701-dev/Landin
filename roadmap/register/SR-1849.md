Work that is no item cannot wait in line for the promotion token: `item.py queue --take NAME` exits 1 while an item holds it, saying "NAME waits in no line" and "`item.py queue` again", and `queue --wait NAME` refuses a name that is no item ("this clone runs no item NAME"). The register sweep of 2026-11-25, its branch's gate passed, met it while R21.61 held the token, and had to poll `--take` from a loop of its own, which AGENTS.md's waiting rules otherwise forbid.

Completion: `queue --take NAME` places work that is no item in line, and `queue --wait NAME` waits for and takes the token for it as it does for an item.
