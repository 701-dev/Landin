`check.py` counts a row's age as the items complete in the working `ROADMAP.md` that were not complete in the commit that filed it. When R19's integration branch took main, four rows filed on main that day (SR-1433, SR-1466, SR-1467, SR-1468) were counted against R19.10, R19.30 and R19.40, items completed on the branch before those rows existed, so the close was refused until each was kept with a `Kept:` line. Met closing R19.40; every window item that takes main meets it again.

A `Kept:` line written in a merge's resolution does not restart its row's count either: `register_ages` finds the line with `git log -G`, which shows no diff for a merge commit. R20.40 met it merging R20.50 into its branch (SR-1432 still said it had stood open through 11 completed items until the line was changed again in an ordinary commit). `--diff-merges=first-parent` would see it, but would make a row or `Kept:` line merged in from a parallel branch look new at the merge and undercount its age.

Completion: A row's age counts only the items completed after it was filed, on the line it was filed on or since the merge that brought it, and a window item taking main is refused for no row the merge alone aged. A `Kept:` line a merge adds on the branch's own side restarts the count, while one merged in from the other parent keeps its own commit's; or the age problem says that a `Kept:` line in a merge's resolution needs a commit of its own.
