D339 never copies a routine that has received a copy itself, and copies whichever call it plans first, so a small routine whose cold path calls another loses its own copy to that one: `hosted/heap`'s `align_up`, a mask with a call to a remainder routine for units that are not powers of two, stayed a call in `host_alloc` because the remainder routine was copied into it. R16.24 wrote the mask into `host_alloc` instead. The weight is also counted as lowering made a routine, so `if unit & (unit - 1) == 0 then first & (unit - 1) else first % unit end if` weighs more than 64 while each half alone weighs under 39.

Kept: R19's breaking window changes the language and no backend, allocator or inliner, so nothing since it was filed has measured or touched what it records.

Completion: Copies planned callees first, or a routine that received copies copied as it then is, within D339's growth bound, with the heap's `align_up` copied as written.

Kept: aged past ten by R20's window items; R20.11 lowered one conversion on each backend and changed no allocator, selection, block layout, inliner or build report, and R20.40 and R20.50 changed the library and the documents, so nothing has measured or touched what it records.
