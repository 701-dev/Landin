A new measured workload has no baselines. Off `main` the gate's measuring jobs report its figures as unrecorded and pass, but `main`'s own gate refuses them, so they must be recorded from that run with `measure.py record --from-run RUN` and committed before the promotion: a second push and a second complete gate. The figures for Windows, Darwin, FreeBSD and the firmware targets come from lanes no single local host runs, so they cannot be recorded before the first push. Met by R22.21 and R22.22 in batch-1, which each needed a push for this alone; under batches the push is the batch's. `docs/measurement.md` does not say that a new workload costs a push, or how to keep it to one.

Completion: a batch's promotion recording the unrecorded figures of the run it promotes on, or a way to measure every target's figures before the first push, or `docs/measurement.md` saying the cost and its route.
