R22.11 states `core/complex`'s errors as measured, and three are wider than the rest: `tanh` and `tan` reach 6.1 ulp in a part, `sinh`'s error counted twice in Kahan's `s²`; and `pow`'s error grows with |w·log z|, 19 ulp of the larger part for parts of `z` up to 4 and of `w` up to 2, since `w·log z` is rounded before its exponential. `tanh` can take its parts from one `expm1(2|x|)`, whose errors then cancel, and `pow` needs `log` and `arg` to twice the precision, as a hi-lo pair, with the product formed exactly.

Completion: `tanh` and `tan` within 3 ulp a part, and `pow` within a few ulp of the larger part independent of |w·log z| over the same domains, each measured as the others are.
