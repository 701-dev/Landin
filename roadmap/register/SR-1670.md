Four old forms of R20's breaking list are refused without saying that the list changed them, and `refine fix` rewrites none of them, though the list calls their rewrites mechanical. A call of `stacks.round_over`, `fake.network_over_turns`, `io.make_temporary` (and the `file_system` entry), `mem.transfer` or `random.shuffle` written in the old parameter order is refused as a type mismatch at the argument now out of place, which does not name D1954's order, and the swap is not offered. A label `entries:` on `round_over` or `events.over` is refused as a label no parameter has, with no respelling to `fibres`. `io.variable` written as a type, the record's old alias, now names a routine and is refused as no type. `posix.words` given a library atom is refused by the argument's type, not pointed at `system.words`. D2129's table refuses a name that fails to resolve, and each of these resolves. Met applying the list in R20.60.

Completion: each of the four refused naming what replaced it, with the swap, the label and the type as exact fixes where the old form is told apart by its types, pinned by a negative fixture each, or the reason recorded.
