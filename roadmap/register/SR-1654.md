D2100 weighs a routine with a loop as lowering made it, since copying fannkuch-redux's `reverse_prefix` into its caller's loop cost 3% to 7% more cycles for 8.7% fewer instructions. The same rule leaves fasta's `select_symbol` and `make_cumulative` calls, whose copies ran fasta 4.7% fewer instructions and about 12% fewer cycles (shared host, noisy), so the loop rule gives up a gain to avoid a loss; neither was measured on a quiet host or on arm64.

Completion: A rule that tells the two apart from measurement (trip counts, a loop inside a loop, branch behaviour), with both workloads measured in cycles.

Kept: R21.30, which took it past ten items, added Windows debug information and changed neither `Landin.IR.Inlining`'s bound nor a hosted workload.
