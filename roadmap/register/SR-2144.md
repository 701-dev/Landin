`core/math` has no constant for infinity or for NaN: `is_nan` reads one, but a program that wants to write one spells it as an arithmetic it hopes folds, `1.0 / 0.0` or `0.0 / 0.0`, or reaches for the bit pattern. A library item met it beside its own work. Reported to R22.32 by the roadmap manager, unfiled until then.

Completion: `core/math` names a positive infinity and a quiet NaN for each floating type, documented with its sign and payload, with a fixture that round-trips each through `is_nan` and comparison.
