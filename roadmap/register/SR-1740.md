An outcome that never ends holds the whole test program until the job's own limit: R21.20's `runtime/events-network-limits` waited for an accept that QEMU's user emulation never limited, its socket limit not reading back, and `rv64-qemu-1` was cancelled after 60 minutes in gate run 45285990985 with its log ending at outcome 486 of 487, naming neither the fixture nor the wait. The Windows lane already gives each outcome 60 seconds and names the one that did not finish.

Completion: the runtime fixtures' runner ends an outcome past a limit of its own and reports it by name, on every lane, so a hang is one failed outcome rather than a cancelled job.
