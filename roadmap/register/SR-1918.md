A count incremented at most once a step of a counted loop keeps its overflow check, though it can never pass the loop's own count: fasta's `select_symbol` adds a comparison's flag to `index` from zero, once a step, over `lenof cumulative - 1` steps, and still tests the carry of every addition, `jc` on x86-64 and `adds` with `b.cs` on arm64. R16.41 left the loop at eight x86-64 instructions and eight arm64 ones; the check is one of each, 142.5 M of fasta 2,500,000's instructions, 5.0% on x86-64 at speed after R16.41 and 5.4% on arm64, measured by the loop's step count rather than a patch. Proving it needs the count's bound related to the index's, an invariant the simplifier does not keep.

Completion: the check of such a count proved and removed on every target, measured.
