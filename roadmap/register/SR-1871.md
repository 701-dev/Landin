R22.24 made `timing.py run` refuse to time the RP2350's Arm port, `rp2350`, until its boot check has put the cores on Arm, naming the `run.py --board=rp2350 --mode=boot` step and the `rp2350_hazard3` boot check that restores the resting image. The run does not do either itself, because a timing bundle carries neither the port's fixed boot image nor the probehive runtime the boot check needs; so `timing.py remote` with no `--board`, which times every port in turn, refuses `rp2350` on a board at rest, and timing both Pico 2 ports by hand still takes two boot checks run around it.

A hand run leaves the board the same way, and the gate's leg does not recover from it: on 2026-11-26 R22.13 re-recorded the Arm port with `run.py --board=rp2350 --mode=boot` and `--mode=ram` (14:05-14:13Z) and nothing after them, and the `board-pico2` leg of boards run 45838122805, at 14:34Z, failed at its first image, `rp2350_hazard3` ram, with "attach without reset ... Timeout during DMI access"; its re-run passed. Met by the gate-watcher change.

Completion: `timing.py run` for `rp2350` switches the cores to Arm and back to the resting image itself, or `remote` runs the boot checks around it; and a run on the Arm port, by hand or in the gate, leaves the resting image behind it, or the `rp2350_hazard3` leg restores it before its first attach.
