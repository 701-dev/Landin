x86-64 stores an `f64` to memory through the frame and a general register: n-body's `advance` writes each velocity as `movsd %xmm0,-0x128(%rbp)`, `mov -0x128(%rbp),%rdx`, `mov %rdx,(%r9)` where `movsd %xmm0,(%r9)` would do, six such stores a pair, about 120 M of the 1.53 G instructions of 1,000,000 steps at speed, some 8%, and `times_a` of spectral-norm stores its sum the same way. arm64 stores the float register directly (`str d22, [x7]`). The same routine keeps `dt` in the frame and computes two addresses it overwrites before the call to `sqrt_f64`.

Completion: a float stored from its float register on x86-64, n-body's instructions measured before and after.
