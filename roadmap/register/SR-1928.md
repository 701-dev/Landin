`core/big/limbs`'s division is Knuth's Algorithm D and its decimal text divides by 10^9 a pass at a time, so both grow with the square of the length, while multiplication is Toom-3's. At a million bits a division of two million bits by one million takes 2.1 s on x86-64 against 58 ms for a product (compiler/tests/big/README.md), and decimal text is slower still.

Completion: divide-and-conquer division, Burnikel and Ziegler's or Newton's reciprocal, and divide-and-conquer radix conversion over it, each above a threshold timed as the multiplication's were, with the `big-limbs-products` fixture's shapes extended past the new thresholds.
