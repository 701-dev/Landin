On Cortex-M0's 32 KiB reference a program that adds, compares, multiplies or divides `core/big` integers fits, but one that both multiplies and divides does not: `big-integer-roots` is 36,588 bytes at `size/auto`, `big-integer-modular` 39,508 and the rational fixtures 43,128 to 50,968, so their corpus rows record profile limits and they execute on the `mps2-an500` lane instead. Multiplication through Toom-3 is 9,724 bytes of the limb layer's 20,310.

Completion: those fixtures fit the reference at `size/auto`, by smaller code for the routines they share (a multiplication that leaves Toom-3 and Karatsuba out below a length a firmware program reaches, or tighter Thumb code), and their profile limits are removed.
