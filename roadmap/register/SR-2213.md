`build-modes` runs within a few minutes of its 30-minute `timeout-minutes` on a normal runner, so a slow runner cancels it, and the whole gate with it. Shards are dealt by request digest and take 17 to 21 minutes each: gate 46040665668 took 17.1 to 20.3, but r16-44's gate 46033999803 had a shard at 29.3. Met by batch-1's gate 46067889013: build-modes-1 and -2 were cancelled at 30 minutes, 93% through 7459 requests. Shard 3 kept the previous gate's pace (90% at 17.6 minutes against 17.1), and shard 1 ran 1.7 times slower at every quartile with the same request count, so the cause was the runner, not the tree. Rerunning the two jobs in attempt 2 cost a further half hour of gate time.

Completion: a margin the job keeps on a slow runner, from a fifth or sixth shard, a limit set from measured durations, or a faster `modes` comparison, and the limit's reason written beside `timeout-minutes`.
