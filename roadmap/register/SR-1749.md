`refine dap` run from WSL sometimes waits more than a minute for `lldb-dap.exe` to answer its first request, and then the session fails as a debugger that said nothing. Met by R21.30 on GitHub's `windows-2025` runner under WSL 1: the first `lldb-dap.exe` the job started from WSL gave no answer within 60 seconds in three of six fresh jobs (gate runs 45283447450 twice, and a probe), while every later start in the same job answered in about two seconds, eleven times; the native `lldb.exe` sessions before it had passed. Neither LLDB's log variable nor the session's environment made the difference. The gate's job now starts `lldb-dap.exe` once from WSL before the session and prints how long it took, which routes around the stall without finding it: Windows' scanning of a program WSL starts, or WSL's interop starting up, are the suspects, and `refine dap`'s own limit for an adapter's first answer, one minute, is what a user would meet.

Completion: the cause is known, from the warm-up's timings and LLDB's log the job keeps, and either `refine dap` waits for an adapter's first answer as long as that takes, saying so, or the job drops its warm-up.
