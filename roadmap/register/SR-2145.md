A fixture's `main` cannot be run on firmware outside the corpus harness: the lanes link each fixture into the harness's own image and report through it, and nothing a fixture author has runs one program alone on an emulated board to see what it does. A library item met it fitting its fixtures to the firmware lanes. Reported to R22.32 by the roadmap manager, unfiled until then.

Completion: one documented command builds and runs a single runtime fixture on each firmware lane's emulator, printing its output and status, or the reason recorded.
