A train takes only items: `item.py merge-main HOLDER --with OTHER` refuses a name this clone runs no item of, so a gated branch that is no item, such as a register sweep, a pin clean-up or a window's claim, still holds the token alone with `queue --take` and lands by hand. The study of 2026-11-25 counted four such promotions in two days, 5 to 36 minutes of token time each. Its design gives the next step: `item.py train start|push|boards|promote|finish`, a session holding the token on a branch `train/STAMP` of its own, whose members may be plain branches accepted on a passed complete push gate of their pushed head, recorded in `landin-items/trains/NAME.json`.

Completion: work that is no item rides a train on its passed gate, and the train lands with the same evidence an item's does.
