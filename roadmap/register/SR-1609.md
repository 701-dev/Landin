A `match` cannot name a declared atom set as one arm. R20.40 met it writing `system.words` (D2008): its argument is `posix.errno | failure.reason`, and the routine wants to pass the errnos to `posix.words` and the library's atoms to `failure.words`. `_ (rest):` binds what is left as a set of its own, but only after every other atom is named, so the routine names each of `failure.reason`'s forty atoms in an arm of its own, `mem.full: failure.words(mem.full)`, and passes the rest to `posix.words`. Nothing in [1210] or the refusals says how to split a union of two sets, and a reader would try an arm that names the set, `posix.errno (number):`, which is L0333 twice, "an atom arm has no payload to bind" and "this atom is not a member of the matched set", neither saying how to split the union.

Completion: an arm may name a declared atom set, binding the subject narrowed to it, and `system.words` is two arms; or [1210] says that a union is split by naming one side's atoms and `_ (rest):`, and L0333 for an arm naming a set says so.
