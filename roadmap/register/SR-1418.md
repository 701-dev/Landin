A routine with a named result, `-> (sum: sha256.digest)`, beside `memory := region.over(ledger, parent)` and `defer region.release(ledger, parent)` is refused at the `defer` with L0315, "the call may change its storage while `memory` still views it", naming `memory` and the release but not the use that keeps `memory` live there; the same routine writing the digest through `inout sum` compiles, and `tree.of_tree`, with the same three lines and a named result, compiles too. Reproduced on a refine built from aa125dca2 by giving refine-build's `run_step` (`steps/steps.ldn`, at d453914) its named result back; its result is assigned from calls that take `memory`, `store.admit(host, parent, memory, ...)`, though a digest is a value. Met writing refine-build (friction entry 20).

The same refusal comes without a named result: a `begin` block that makes `memory := region.over(ledger, parent)` with `defer region.release(ledger, parent)` is L0315 at the `defer`, where the same two lines at a routine's top are accepted (friction entry 26); moving the block's body into a routine avoided it.

Completion: the refusal naming the use that keeps the view live, and, if a value result taken from a call that was passed the view is what holds it, that hold removed as a false positive.
