A push's `--all` watch ends at its 7,200-second limit while the run's jobs still wait for runners: R22.42's run 46007084959 had 27 jobs queued after three hours beside other items' gates. `item.py gate ITEM --watch` then prints "already watched" and the finished watch's `--wait` command, which returns at once, so the run cannot be followed again through `item.py`. The watch was rearmed by hand as `item.py run ITEM NAME --limit 14400 -- python3 scripts/gate_watch.py --run RUN --all`. Met in R22.42 following its second push.

Completion: A watch that ended at its limit without the run's verdict is started again by `item.py gate --watch`, or the watch's limit follows the run rather than a fixed two hours.
