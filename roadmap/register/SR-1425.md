A loop is laid out with its test at the head, its body, then a block that steps the counter and jumps back: every iteration takes two jumps, and SipHash-1-3's and SHA-256's loops reach the step through a `jmp` to a block that is one `add` and a `jmp`. A branch's emission also leaves an unconditional `jmp` after another that nothing reaches (`siphash` at speed-auto has three). Measured in R16.24 on x86-64; arm64's layout is the same.

Kept: R19's breaking window changes the language and no backend, allocator or inliner, so nothing since it was filed has measured or touched what it records.

Completion: A loop tested at its foot, the step placed where the body falls into it, and no unreachable jump, measured on the hosted workloads.

Kept: aged past ten by R20's window items; R20.11 lowered one conversion on each backend and changed no allocator, selection, block layout, inliner or build report, and R20.40 and R20.50 changed the library and the documents, so nothing has measured or touched what it records.
