A `Kept:` line written while resolving a merge does not restart its row's count. R20.40 met it merging R20.50 into its branch: SR-1432 took a `Kept:` line in the merge's resolution, and `python3 check.py` still said it "has stood open through 11 completed items", with nothing pointing at the merge. `register_ages` finds the line with `git log -G`, which shows no diff for a merge commit, so the line counts only once it is changed again in an ordinary commit, which is what R20.40 did. `--diff-merges=first-parent` would see it, but it would also make a row or a `Kept:` line merged in from a parallel branch look new at the merge and undercount its age, so the reader was left as it is.

Completion: a `Kept:` line a merge adds on the branch's own side restarts the count, while one merged in from the other parent keeps its own commit's; or the age problem says that a `Kept:` line in a merge's resolution needs a commit of its own.
