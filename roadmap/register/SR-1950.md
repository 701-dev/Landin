`core/math`'s `f32` sine, cosine and tangent reduce their argument in `f64`, as msun's `f32` routines do, so an `f32` program on a target without a double float unit links libgcc's double soft float: about 8 KiB on Cortex-M0. R22.11 met it as the margin by which `core/complex`'s `f32` trigonometric and hyperbolic fixtures miss Cortex-M0's 32 KiB, 2.4 to 3.4 KiB each, and `exp` at `f32` fits only at the size and speed profiles.

Completion: an `f32` trigonometric routine that reduces in `f32` arithmetic, or in integers, with its error unchanged and its Cortex-M0 size measured before and after.

Kept: aged past ten by R16.44's close, which proves overflow checks in the simplifier and changed nothing in `core/math`'s argument reduction; its activation, R22.80's measured basis, has not come.

Kept: batch-1 (R22.21, R22.22, R22.29, R22.34, R22.41 to R22.45) changed neither `core/math`'s trigonometric argument reduction nor anything of R22.80's basis, which has not run.
