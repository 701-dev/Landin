A Cortex-M program that compiles one pattern with `regex.lean()` and searches it is 150,356 bytes of Thumb text at `size/auto` (R22.60's `runtime/library-shared-regex`), past the 32 KiB ARMv6-M profile: 50 KB of it are the `core/mem` and `core/vec` routines instantiated once per item type, a dozen types each with its own `reserve_storage`, `copy_prefix`, `admit` and `push`, 25 KB the parser, 18 KB the compiler and 30 KB the four engines. The largest routine, `parse_posix`, is 4.5 KB of failure paths around the `try` of each range it adds. The DFA and the one-pass DFA are linked into every program, though a lean firmware search could do with the Pike VM.

Completion: a lean program of one pattern and one search measured again after either sharing the container routines between item types of one size or selecting the engines through the options as D2676 selects the tables, with the sizes recorded.

Kept: batch-1 met its activation without taking it: R22.44 and R22.45 measured firmware code size on Cortex-M0 (D3096, D3097; the caseless tables and ASCII forms), and R22.41 split its firmware fixtures to fit 32 KiB. Kept for the register sweep after batch-1, which takes it.
