A binding named `any`, `any: net.address = net.unspecified(net.ipv4, 0)`, is refused with L0101, "`any` requires one concept name", pointing at the colon, and its note explains `any C` erasure. Nothing says that `any` is a keyword and cannot be a name, which L0100 says plainly for `from`: "`from` is a keyword, and no keyword is available as a name", with a longer spelling to use. Met writing `runtime/network-multicast`, where the name read naturally for "any interface".

Completion: a keyword met where a binding's or a parameter's name is expected, `any` among them, is refused as L0100 refuses `from`, naming the keyword and suggesting another spelling, with a negative fixture.
