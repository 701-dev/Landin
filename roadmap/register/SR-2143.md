Swapping two `big.integer` handles is refused with L0315, a place changed while a view derived from it is still in use, and `core/big` has no public exchange that would do it for the caller. A library item met it beside its own work and wrote the exchange out by hand; the refusal names the view but not what to write instead. Reported to R22.32 by the roadmap manager, unfiled until then.

Completion: `core/big` exchanges two values in place through a public function, or L0315's note for a swap of two handles says what to write, with a fixture either way.
