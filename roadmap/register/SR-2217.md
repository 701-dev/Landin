On `windows-11-arm`, llvm-mingw's LLDB sometimes runs on through a `thread step-inst` rather than stopping after the one instruction, in about one session in 47; the lane runs the session again when it loses a step (D2970), and the cause inside LLDB is unknown. Met by R22.28; the row that recorded it, `SR-1940`, was deleted, and the behaviour stands.

Completion: the cause found and fixed or reported upstream with a reproducer, or a pin under which the session no longer loses a step over a measured number of runs, and the retry removed.
