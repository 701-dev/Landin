A `core/args` table cannot say that two arguments come together or exclude each other: the examples page's tree copy takes two paths or none, and with `FROM` and `TO` both optional its `main` refuses one path alone by writing its own `missing argument TO` and the usage line, a workaround beside the parser (D3098). Clap's `requires` and `conflicts_with`, and Swift's argument groups, say it in the declaration. Met by R22.44 moving the tree copy on 2026-11-27.

Completion: a row can name the rows it needs or excludes, `parse` refuses the words that break it with a refusal `explain` says, and the tree copy declares it rather than checking by hand.
