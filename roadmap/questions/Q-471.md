# Q-471 — Does R16 get an item for the inliner's weights, taking SR-1654 and SR-1739?

Asked by the register sweep of 2026-11-28 on 2026-11-28.

Two open rows wait for the next R16 item that changes `Landin.IR.Inlining`'s
weights, and no planned item does. `SR-1654`: D2100 never copies a routine
with a loop, which gives up fasta's gain of about 12% in cycles to avoid
fannkuch-redux's loss; batch-1 met its activation again in k-nucleotide's
loop over `core/map`'s `at` and spectral-norm's looping products. `SR-1739`:
the size profile keeps a routine that only forwards to another as a call,
which cost every hosted Linux image about 200 bytes after R21.20 and about
300 more above x86-64-v1 after R21.70; the baselines record the growth.
R16's continuous items are added when one is taken up or split off, and
adding one is a new roadmap item, so the choice is yours.

**Options:**

1. Add a planned R16 continuous item, "The inliner's weights from
   measurement", depending on R16.10, which takes both rows: the size
   profile copies a forwarding routine, and the loop rule tells
   fannkuch-redux's case from fasta's by trip count or nesting, measured in
   cycles on x86-64 and arm64 on those four programs.
2. Add no item; both rows stay open until an item that changes the weights
   for another reason takes them.

**Recommendation:** option 1. Both rows are about the same weights, both
have a measured cost, and since batch-1 k-nucleotide and spectral-norm
meet the loop rule beside fasta and fannkuch-redux; the rows keep being
met and not taken.

**Default until answered:** no item is added; both rows stay open with
their activations as they are.

**What waits on the answer:** nothing else; the item, if added, waits for
the next free R16 slot like the others.

**Answer (2026-11-28, the maintainer):** option 1, "Add it". R16.47, "The
inliner's weights from measurement", is planned on R16.10 and takes
`SR-1654` and `SR-1739`.
