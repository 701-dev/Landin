# Q-144 — Should fibres and threads share one task concept, and a fibre group one erased provider?

Asked by R20.10 on 2026-11-22.

The inventory's second question. `fibre.task` and `thread.job` have the
identical entry, `run: (self: ptr mut T) -> none`, and each has its own
`launch` bridge. `fibre.group_value` keeps both an `any scheduler` and an
`any turns` with a `takes_turns` flag, because `turns is scheduler` and
yet `any turns` does not convert to `any scheduler`. Holding one provider
needs that conversion, a language change; R19's window, where the
inventory would have filed it, has closed.

**Options:**

1. One task concept in `core` (`core/fibre`'s `task`, which
   `hosted/thread` uses), one `launch`. The group keeps its two fields
   until `any` converts along a concept's parents; that conversion is
   filed as a register row for a later language item.
2. One task concept, and the conversion of `any C` to `any P` for a parent
   `P` of `C` taken into R20's window as a language change, so the group
   holds one `any scheduler` and drops its flag.
3. Keep `task` and `job` apart, since a thread is an execution context and
   a fibre is not (D446); share only the bridge.

**Recommendation:** option 2. The conversion is small, additive and the
reading [1690] already suggests; a breaking window is the place for it,
and it removes the flag rather than recording why it stays.

**Default until answered:** one task concept in `core`; the group's two
fields stay, and the parent conversion is filed as a row (option 1).

**What waits on the answer:** R20.30's task entry, and whether R20 gains a
language item for the conversion.

**Answer (2026-11-22, the maintainer):** option 2. Recorded as D1949.
