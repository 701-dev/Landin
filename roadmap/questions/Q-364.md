# Q-364 — How long does the selection run in shadow mode before R22.37 applies it?

Asked by the gate-time study of 2026-11-26 on 2026-11-26.

The maintainer answered on 2026-11-26 that the selection runs in shadow
mode first: R22.36 computes the selected gate on every run and skips
nothing, so the lanes it would have skipped are measured against the
complete gates, and the maintainer sees that miss rate before R22.37
applies it, which is why R22.37 says `Promotion: maintainer`. The
length is still his. It sets how many defects the rate rests on: the
gate showed 172 defects from 1 to 26 November, about 45 a week, in some
220 complete gates a week at that month's busiest.

**Options:**

1. About 200 complete gates from the shadow selection's first run.
2. Four weeks from its first run.
3. Whichever ends later of 200 complete gates and 40 defects in the
   candidates.

**Recommendation:** option 3: a count of gates follows how busy the
runners are, a week or less at November's pace, and the rate should
rest on enough defects to tell two missed a week from ten.

**Default until answered:** about 200 complete gates from the shadow
selection's first run.

**What waits on the answer:** R22.37's start; nothing in R22.36, which
records from its first run whatever the length.

**Answer (2026-11-27, the maintainer):** through the manager's chat,
option 1, the default: the selection runs in shadow mode for about 200
complete gates from its first run, and R22.37 starts once they are run
and the maintainer has seen the miss rate. Closed.
