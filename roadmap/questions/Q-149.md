# Q-149 — What order do a library routine's parameters take?

Asked by R20.10 on 2026-11-22.

The inventory's seventh question. R13.11's conventions (D268-D271) and
the later ones in `core/README.md` fix parameter names (`memory`, `host`,
`state`, `self`) but no order; "subject first" appears only in D945.
Most modules already put the subject first.

**Options:**

1. Subject first, then capabilities (`memory`, `host`, entropy), then the
   other inputs; a `caller` parameter, where one exists, last.
2. Capabilities first, then the subject, then inputs, so every routine
   that allocates starts with its allocator.
3. No order rule; names alone.

**Recommendation:** option 1, what most modules do, so the breaking list
stays short.

**Default until answered:** the inventory's: subject first, then
capabilities, then inputs.

**What waits on the answer:** R20.20's convention and its departures on
the list.
